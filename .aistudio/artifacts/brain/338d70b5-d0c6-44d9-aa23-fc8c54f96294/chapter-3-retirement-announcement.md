# Chapter 3: The Retirement Announcement That Shocked Football

![The Retirement Announcement That Shocked Football](Retirement.jpeg)

**MetLife Stadium, East Rutherford, New Jersey — June 26, 2016**

In the humid bowels of MetLife Stadium on the night of June 26, 2016, Lionel Messi delivered words that sent shockwaves across global football:

> *"For me, the national team is over. I've done all I can. It hurts not to be a champion... I tried so hard, it was what I wanted most. It didn't happen for me, and I think this is for everyone's good."*

This photograph captures the raw agony of that night—the four lost finals, the crushing burden of expectation, and the historic turning point that paved the path for his return and ultimate coronation in Qatar.
